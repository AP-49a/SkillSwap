How SkillSwap Works

A seamless credit loop built for collaborative learning.

1

Join & Get 10 Credits

Create your profile, detail your expertise and language capabilities, and receive a welcome bonus of 10 credits.

2

Book a Mentor

Search through listings. Book slots using your credits. Credits are stored securely until the class is complete.

3

List your skills (e.g. coding, music, cooking). Teach sessions for others, earn credits, and repeat the cycle.
