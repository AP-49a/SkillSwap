About SkillSwap

Our Philosophy

Knowledge is currency. In traditional education, learning requires monetary resources that not everyone has. SkillSwap was founded on a simple concept: everyone has something to teach, and everyone has something to learn. By utilizing a virtual credit exchange, we democratize mentoring and learning.

The Credit System Loop

When you create an account, we grant you 10 welcome credits. You can browse our marketplace of engineering, design, language, and creative tutorials to book a 1-on-1 session. Credits are deducted and securely locked until completion. When the slot finishes, the credits are released to the instructor. If you run out of credits, simply list your own skills, book sessions as an instructor, and earn credits from other learners.

Trust and Safety

We enforce transparency through user-driven profiles, ratings, and reviews. Every session is booked explicitly using date slots and timeslots, and learners can leave comprehensive feedback and rating scores on skills they've completed.
